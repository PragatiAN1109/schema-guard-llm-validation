HC-003 discharge_after_admission critical
Fields: admission_date, discharge_date
▲
⚡ Why This Matters

Negative length-of-stay is clinically impossible and breaks DRG calculation. UB-04 claims with LOS<0 are rejected by all CMS-certified billing systems.

Example violation

admission: 2024-08-15
discharge: 2024-08-08
LOS = −7 days

Regulatory reference

NUBC UB-04 FL6/FL16 · CMS CoP §482.24(c)(2)(vii)
